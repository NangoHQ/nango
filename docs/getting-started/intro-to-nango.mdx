## What is Nango?

Nango is the tool calling layer for AI agents.

It makes your agents powerful with actions, and smart with context, across [1,300+ APIs](/integrations/overview).

Your agent gets a catalog of 10,000+ pre-built tools with built-in auth, Nango-managed OAuth apps, a token-efficient execution engine for fast and secure tool calls, and built-in governance, observability, and audit logs for every call.

## Why it matters

**Launch hundreds of integrations today.**  
Pre-built tools and auth work out of the box, so you add APIs as your customers ask for them, without building each one.

**Your agents are only as good as their tools.**  
Nango tunes its tools on hundreds of millions of production runs. It learns which tasks agents actually run, then shapes the tools, their inputs and their responses around those tasks. It adds plans for multi-step work and instructions for undocumented API quirks. Your agents complete more tasks with fewer tokens and errors, and they get better with every run. Official MCP servers and hand-rolled tools don’t learn from production.

Nango runs on Nango Cloud, in your own cloud account (BYOC), or self-hosted.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step id="add-nango" title="Add Nango to your agent">
    One integration gives your agent access to every API in Nango: [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart)
  </Step>

  <Step id="access-apis" title="Your agent acts across 1,300+ APIs">
    Your users connect their accounts through Nango Auth. Your agent searches the catalog, finds the right tools, and calls them on the user's behalf, without ever seeing credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step id="observe-control" title="You control and observe every call">
    Scope what each agent can see and do for each user. See logs and a full audit trail for every tool call, across all APIs, in one dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
