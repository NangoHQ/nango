## Overview

To authenticate with Screvi, you will need:
1. **API Key** - A scoped key that grants access to your Screvi library: highlights, sources, saved articles and tags.

This guide will walk you through generating your **API Key** within Screvi.

### Prerequisites:

- You must have a Screvi account; a free trial is enough to generate a key.

### Instructions:

#### Step 1: Generating your API key

1. Sign in to your Screvi account at [app.screvi.com](https://app.screvi.com).
2. Navigate to the [API settings](https://app.screvi.com/settings/api) page.
3. Select **Create API key** and give it a name that identifies the integration.
4. Choose its scopes. `read` is enough to list and search; add `write` if the integration needs to save articles, create highlights or edit tags.
5. Copy the key. It begins with `sk_live_` and is shown only once, so store it before leaving the page.

#### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

Once you have your **API Key**:
1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Screvi.
2. Enter your **API Key** in its respective field.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Screvi.
