## Overview

To authenticate with Informa, you will need:
1. **Username** - Your Informa API EMPRESAS username.
2. **Password** - Your Informa API EMPRESAS password.

### Prerequisites:

- You must be an Informa D&amp;B customer with API EMPRESAS access to `https://services.informa.es`.
- Your contract must include the packaged products you intend to call (for example `INFORME_FINANCIERO`).

### Instructions:

#### Step 1: Obtaining your Informa credentials
1. Sign up for / enable **API EMPRESAS** with Informa ([overview](https://www.informa.es/necesidades/api-empresas)).
2. Obtain your API **username** and **password** from your Informa account administrator or contract.
3. Keep the password secret; Nango stores it encrypted with the connection.

#### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

Once you have your credentials:
1. Open the Connect UI form for Informa.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Submit the form. Nango will attach `username` and `password` headers on proxy requests to `https://services.informa.es`.

You are now connected to Informa. Call `GET /api/get-product` through the Nango proxy (see the Informa integration docs for products, formats, and error handling).
