## Overview

To authenticate with Belender, you will need:
1. **API Hostname** - Production (`api.belender.net`) or QA (`api.qa.belender.net`).
2. **Email** - Your Belender account email.
3. **Password** - Your Belender account password.

### Prerequisites:

- You must have a registered Belender account with API access.
- You need Belender **package IDs** (UUIDs) for the packs you will request (they differ between QA and production).

### Instructions:

#### Step 1: Choosing your API hostname
1. Use `api.belender.net` for production workloads.
2. Use `api.qa.belender.net` for QA / staging.

#### Step 2: Entering your Belender credentials
1. Open the Connect UI form for Belender.
2. Enter the **API Hostname** (without `https://`).
3. Enter your Belender **Email** and **Password**.
4. Submit the form. Nango will call `POST /login` and store the returned `access_token`.

You are now connected to Belender. Use the Nango proxy for document flows (`/requests/...`, `/digital-power/send-job`), Cl@ve PIN / SMS steps, and document / fast-json retrieval (see the Belender integration docs).
