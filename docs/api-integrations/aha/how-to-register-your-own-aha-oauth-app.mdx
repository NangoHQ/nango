This guide shows you how to register your own app with Aha! to obtain your OAuth credentials (client ID & secret). These are required to let your users grant your app access to their Aha! account.

<Steps>
  <Step id="open-oauth-applications" title="Open your OAuth applications">
    Sign in to Aha!, click your profile, and go to **Settings** ⚙️ -> **Personal** -> **Developer**. Then open the **OAuth applications** tab.

    <Note>
    Account administrators can turn off new OAuth app registrations. If you don't see the **Register OAuth application** button, ask your Aha! administrator.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step id="register-application" title="Register an OAuth application">
    Click **Register OAuth application** and fill in:
    - **Name** — a name your users will recognize.
    - **Redirect URI(s)** — `https://api.nango.dev/oauth/callback`

    Click **Create**.

    <Tip>
    An Aha! OAuth app can be used by any Aha! user, not only users in your own account.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step id="copy-credentials" title="Copy your credentials">
    Open your application and copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Enter them in your Aha! integration in Nango.

    Aha! has no OAuth scopes, so you don't need to configure any.
  </Step>
  <Step id="next" title="Next">
    Follow the [_Quickstart_](/getting-started/quickstart) to connect your first account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details, see [Aha!'s OAuth2 documentation](https://www.aha.io/api/oauth2).
