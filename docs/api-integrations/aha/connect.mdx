## Overview

To authenticate with Aha!, you need:

1. **Aha! Subdomain** — the subdomain of your Aha! account.

This guide will show you how to find it.

### Prerequisites

- An Aha! account.

### Step 1: Find your subdomain

1. Sign in to Aha!.
2. Look at the address in your browser. It looks like `https://yourcompany.aha.io`.
3. Your subdomain is the part before `.aha.io`. In this example, it's `yourcompany`.

### Step 2: Enter your subdomain in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Aha!.
2. Enter your **Aha! Subdomain**.
3. Click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to Aha! to authorize access.

<img src="/api-integrations/aha/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Aha!.
