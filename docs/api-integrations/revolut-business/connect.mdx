## Overview

To authenticate with Revolut Business, you need:

1. **Environment** — whether you're connecting to Revolut's production or sandbox account.

This guide will walk you through selecting your environment and connecting your Revolut Business account.

### Prerequisites

- A Revolut Business account.

### Step 1: Selecting your environment

Use the table below to find the correct hostnames for the environment where you registered your certificate:

| Environment | Authorization Host | API Host |
|-------------|---------------------------------------|------------------------|
| Production | `business.revolut.com` | `b2b.revolut.com` |
| Sandbox | `sandbox-business.revolut.com` | `sandbox-b2b.revolut.com` |

### Step 2: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

Once you've identified your environment:

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Revolut Business.
2. Select your **Environment**.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/revolut-business/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Revolut Business.
