## Overview

To authenticate with Court Rules, you need:
1. **API key** - A key shown on the dashboard of the Court Rules console. Keys look like `crm_prod_...`.

This guide will walk you through getting your **API key** from the Court Rules console.

### Prerequisites

- A Court Rules console login. Sign in with a Google or email account at [console.courtrules.app](https://console.courtrules.app).

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Get your Court Rules API key

1. Log in to the [Court Rules console](https://console.courtrules.app).
2. On the dashboard, find the **API Key** card and click **Copy**.

<Tip>Each key has per-minute limits: 300 requests per minute on court, judge, rule and holiday lookups, 60 on compliance checks and 5 on document classification. When a limit is exceeded, Court Rules returns a `429` with a `Retry-After` header, which Nango uses as the retry delay. Email [api@courtrules.app](mailto:api@courtrules.app) if you need higher limits.</Tip>

#### Step 2: Enter your API key in the Connect UI

Once you have your **API key**:

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Court Rules.
2. Enter your **API key** in the field.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Court Rules.
