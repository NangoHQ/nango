This guide shows you how to create a TeamViewer app to get your OAuth credentials (client ID & secret). Your users then authorize your app to access their TeamViewer account through the TeamViewer API.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a TeamViewer account">
    If you don't have a TeamViewer account, [sign up](https://login.teamviewer.com/) for one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    1. Log in to the [TeamViewer Management Console](https://login.teamviewer.com/), click your user icon and select **Edit profile**.
    2. Select **Apps**, then click **Create App**.
    3. Enter a name and a description for your app.
    4. Choose whether the app is for your own account or for your company. If it's for company use, only TeamViewer company administrators can authorize it.
    5. Set the redirect URI to `https://api.nango.dev/oauth/callback` (or your own [custom callback URL](/guides/auth/auth-guide#custom-oauth-callback-url-optional)). It must match exactly.
    6. Choose the permissions your integration needs, for example the permission to read account data (`Account.Read`) to read the connected account.
    7. Click **Create**, then copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the credentials in Nango">
    In your TeamViewer integration in Nango, paste the client ID and secret. Leave scopes empty: TeamViewer applies the permissions chosen on the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next">
    Follow the [_Quickstart_](/getting-started/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details, see the [TeamViewer API documentation](https://dl.teamviewer.com/integrate/TeamViewer_API_Documentation.pdf) and [Build a TeamViewer integration](https://www.teamviewer.com/en/global/support/knowledge-base/teamviewer-remote/for-developers/build-a-teamviewer-integration/).
