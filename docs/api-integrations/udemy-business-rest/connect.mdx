## Overview

To connect with the Udemy Business REST API, you need:

1. **Subdomain**: the subdomain of your Udemy Business site.
2. **Account ID**: your Udemy Business account ID, a number.
3. **Client ID**: the client ID of a REST API client in your Udemy Business account.
4. **Client Secret**: the client secret of the same API client.

### Prerequisites

- A Udemy Business account on the Enterprise plan. The APIs are not available on the Team plan.
- Admin access to that account.

### Step 1: Find your subdomain

Your Udemy Business site is at `https://<subdomain>.udemy.com`. Enter the part before `.udemy.com`, such as `acme` for `https://acme.udemy.com`.

### Step 2: Create a REST API client

1. Sign in to your Udemy Business site as an admin.
2. Go to **Manage** > **Settings** > **APIs & integrations**.
3. Create a REST API client.
4. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Keep the secret somewhere safe.

### Step 3: Find your account ID

Udemy shows your account ID, a number, with your REST API details under **APIs & integrations**. Udemy's API documentation also calls it the organization ID, because every REST path includes it: `/api-2.0/organizations/<ACCOUNT-ID>/...`.

### Step 4: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Udemy Business (REST).
2. Enter your **Subdomain** and **Account ID**.
3. Enter your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.
4. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Udemy Business (REST).
