# Overview

To authenticate with SHOPLINE, you need your store's **handle** — the first part of its domain (e.g. `open001` for `open001.myshopline.com`).

### Instructions:

#### Step 1: Finding your store handle

Ask the merchant for their SHOPLINE store URL, or check it yourself if you're connecting your own store. The handle is the subdomain before `.myshopline.com`.

#### Step 2: Enter your store handle in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with SHOPLINE.
2. Enter the **Store Handle**.
3. Submit the form — you'll be redirected to SHOPLINE to authorize the app, then back to complete the connection.

<img src="/api-integrations/shopline-oauth/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to SHOPLINE.
