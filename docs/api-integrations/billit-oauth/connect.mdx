## Overview

To connect your Billit account, choose your environment and sign in to Billit. Your Billit user account must have access to the company you want to connect.

## Step 1: Choose your environment

In the connection form, select the **Environment** your application uses:

- **Sandbox** — `sandbox.billit.be`, for accounts at [Billit Sandbox](https://my.sandbox.billit.be).
- **Production** — `billit.be`, for accounts at [Billit Production](https://my.billit.be).

Sandbox and production use separate accounts. If you are unsure which environment to choose, ask your application's administrator.

## Step 2: Authorize Billit

Select **Authorize**, sign in to Billit in the selected environment, and grant the application access. You will return to the application after authorization.
