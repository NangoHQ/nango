## Overview

To authenticate with Dash, you need:

1. **Dash Subdomain** — the part of your Dash account's address before `.dash.app`.

You then sign in to Dash to approve the connection. This guide will show you how to find your subdomain.

### Prerequisites

- A Dash account. You can [start a free trial](https://www.dash.app/).

### Step 1: Find your Dash subdomain

1. Sign in to Dash.
2. Look at your browser's address bar. Your account's address looks like `https://my-account.dash.app`.
3. Your subdomain is the part between `https://` and `.dash.app`: `my-account` in this example.

### Step 2: Enter your subdomain in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Dash.
2. Enter your **Dash Subdomain** in the designated field.
3. Click **Connect**, then sign in to Dash with an account on that site and approve access.

<img src="/api-integrations/dash/form.png" alt="Dash Connect UI form with the Dash Subdomain field" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Dash.
