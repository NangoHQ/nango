## Overview

HitPay has a **production** and a **sandbox** environment. You choose the environment each time you connect an account.

Production and sandbox use separate OAuth apps with their own client ID and secret. Create one Nango integration per environment, and connect each account to the integration whose credentials match the environment you pick.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Choose your environment

| Environment | Value |
|---|---|
| Production | `hit-pay.com` |
| Sandbox | `sandbox.hit-pay.com` |

Pick the environment that matches the credentials of the integration you are connecting to. A sandbox app's credentials only work with `sandbox.hit-pay.com`, and a production app's credentials only work with `hit-pay.com`.

#### Step 2: Authorize HitPay

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with HitPay.
2. Select your **Environment**.
3. Submit the form, log in to HitPay, and pick the business to connect.

You are now connected to HitPay.
