# Overview

To authenticate with AgencyZoom, you will need:
1. **Username** — Your AgencyZoom account email address.
2. **Password** — Your AgencyZoom account password.

<Note>
Permissions are scoped to the account used to authorize. Use the agency owner's account if your integration needs access to all functionality.
</Note>

### Step 1: Obtain your credentials

Use the email address and password of the AgencyZoom user you want to integrate with. There is no separate API key or app to register — the same credentials used to log in to the AgencyZoom dashboard are used here.

### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with AgencyZoom.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password** in their designated fields.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/agencyzoom/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to AgencyZoom.
