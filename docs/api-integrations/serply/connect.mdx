## Overview

To authenticate with Serply, you will need:
1. **API Key** - The key shown on your Serply dashboard.

This guide will walk you through finding your **API Key** within Serply.

### Prerequisites:

- You must have a Serply account. Sign up at [serply.io](https://serply.io).

#### Step 1: Finding your Serply API key

1. Log in to the [Serply dashboard](https://app.serply.io).
2. Copy the API key shown on the dashboard.

#### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Serply.
2. Enter your **API Key**.
3. Submit the form.

<img src="/api-integrations/serply/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Serply.
