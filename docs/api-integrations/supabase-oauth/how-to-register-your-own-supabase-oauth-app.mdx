This guide shows you how to create an OAuth app in your Supabase organization to get your OAuth credentials (client ID & secret). Your users then authorize your app to manage their own organizations and projects through the Management API.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Supabase account">
    If you don't have a Supabase account, [sign up](https://supabase.com/dashboard/sign-up) for free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an OAuth app">
    1. In your organization's settings, open the [**OAuth Apps**](https://supabase.com/dashboard/org/_/apps) tab.
    2. Click **Publish OAuth app**.
    3. Enter an **Application name** and your **Website URL**.
    4. Under **Authorization callback URLs**, add `https://api.nango.dev/oauth/callback` (or your own [custom callback URL](/guides/auth/auth-guide#custom-oauth-callback-url-optional)).
    5. Under **Application permissions**, set each area your integration needs to **Read** or **Write** (see [scopes](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/integrations/build-a-supabase-oauth-integration/oauth-scopes)): for example **Organizations** and **Projects** to **Read** to list organizations and projects.
    6. Click **Confirm**, then copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. The client secret is shown only once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the credentials in Nango">
    In your Supabase (OAuth) integration in Nango, paste the client ID and secret. Leave scopes empty: Supabase applies the scopes chosen on the OAuth app, and its `scope` parameter is deprecated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next">
    Follow the [_Quickstart_](/getting-started/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>You can change an OAuth app's scopes at any time, but existing users must authorize your app again to get the new scopes.</Note>

For more details, see [Build a Supabase Integration](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/integrations/build-a-supabase-oauth-integration).
