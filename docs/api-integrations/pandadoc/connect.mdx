## Overview

To connect with Pandadoc, you need:

1. **Region** – Which data region your Pandadoc account lives in.

This guide walks you through finding this value.

### Prerequisites

- A Pandadoc account.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Find your region

Pandadoc accounts live in one of two data regions, and each has its own login domain:

- **Global** — you log in at `app.pandadoc.com`. Set **Region** to `pandadoc.com`.
- **EU** — you log in at `app.pandadoc.eu`. Set **Region** to `pandadoc.eu`.

Check the domain in your browser's address bar while logged in to Pandadoc to tell which region you're on.

#### Step 2: Authorize in Nango

1. Open the Nango Connect form.
2. Set the **Region** field to `pandadoc.com` (global) or `pandadoc.eu` (EU), matching your account.
3. Submit the form, log in to Pandadoc, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/pandadoc/form.png" alt="Nango Connect UI form with a required Region dropdown for Pandadoc" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Pandadoc.
