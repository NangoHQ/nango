This guide shows you how to obtain OAuth credentials (client ID & secret) from Omnisend. These are required to let your users grant your app access to their Omnisend account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Omnisend account">
    If you don't have an Omnisend account, sign up at [omnisend.com](https://www.omnisend.com/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply for OAuth credentials">
    Omnisend issues OAuth credentials on request rather than self-serve. Apply through the form linked from [Omnisend's OAuth documentation](https://api-docs.omnisend.com/reference/oauth); Omnisend usually replies within a few business days.
    1. When asked for a redirect URL, give: `https://api.nango.dev/oauth/callback` (or your [custom callback URL](/guides/auth/auth-guide#custom-oauth-callback-url-optional) if you set one).
    2. Once approved, Omnisend sends your **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Enter them in your Omnisend (OAuth) integration in Nango.

    <Note>Omnisend expects the client credentials in the body of the token request; Nango sends them there by default.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your scopes">
    Each Omnisend endpoint lists the scope it needs. Scopes follow the `resource.read` / `resource.write` pattern, for example `contacts.read`, `segments.read`, `campaigns.read` or `email-templates.write`, and are space-separated. Request only what your app uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next">
    Follow the [_Quickstart_](/getting-started/quickstart) to connect your first account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details, see [Omnisend's OAuth documentation](https://api-docs.omnisend.com/reference/oauth). Omnisend's AppMarket listing is a separate step after your integration is built.
