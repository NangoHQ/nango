## Overview

To authenticate with DialMyCalls, you need:
1. **API Key** - The unique API key of your DialMyCalls account.

This guide will walk you through finding your **API Key** in DialMyCalls.

### Prerequisites

- A [DialMyCalls](https://www.dialmycalls.com/signup) account. New accounts get 25 free calls or text messages, no credit card required.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Copy your API key

1. Log in to your [DialMyCalls account](https://www.dialmycalls.com/members/v2/management.php).
2. Open the **Integrations** tab.
3. Open **API Info** and copy your **API Key**.

#### Step 2: Enter your API key in the Connect UI

Once you have your **API Key**:
1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with DialMyCalls.
2. Paste your API key into the **API Key** field.
3. Submit the form. Nango checks the key with DialMyCalls before saving the connection.

<img src="/api-integrations/dialmycalls/form.png" alt="DialMyCalls connection form in Nango's Connect UI" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to DialMyCalls.
