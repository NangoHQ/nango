## Overview

To authenticate with TestMu AI, you need:

1. **Username** — your TestMu AI account username.
2. **Access Key** — the access key of your account.
3. **Data Center Region** — the API host of the data center your account is in.

This guide will show you how to find them.

### Prerequisites

- A TestMu AI account. You can sign up at [testmuai.com](https://www.testmuai.com/register/).

### Step 1: Copy your username and access key

1. Log in to [TestMu AI](https://www.testmuai.com/login/).
2. Open the **Profile** dropdown in the top right and select **Account Settings**.
3. Open the **Password & Security** tab, then **Username and Access Key**.
4. Copy the **Username**. Enter it in the **Username** field.
5. Select the copy button next to **Access Key** to copy the key. Enter it in the **Access Key** field.

<img src="/api-integrations/testmu-ai/copy_access_key.png" alt="TestMu AI Account Settings, Password & Security tab, with the Username and the copy button next to Access Key"/>

### Step 2: Find your data center region

TestMu AI has a US and an EU data center. Accounts in the EU data center (GDPR) sign in at `eu-accounts.lambdatest.com`.

- If you sign in at `eu-accounts.lambdatest.com`, select `eu-api.lambdatest.com`.
- Otherwise, select `api.lambdatest.com`.

### Step 3: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with TestMu AI.
2. Select your **Data Center Region**, then enter your **Username** and **Access Key** in the designated fields.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/testmu-ai/form.png" alt="TestMu AI connection form in the Nango Connect UI" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to TestMu AI.
