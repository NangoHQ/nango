## Overview

To connect with TestMu AI (formerly LambdaTest), you need:

1. **Username** - your TestMu AI username.
2. **Access Key** - your TestMu AI access key.

### Prerequisites

- A TestMu AI account. You can sign up at [testmuai.com](https://www.testmuai.com/).

### Step 1: Find your username and access key

1. Log in to [TestMu AI](https://accounts.lambdatest.com/login).
2. Your **Username** and **Access Key** are shown on the [Automation Dashboard](https://automation.lambdatest.com/) and on your [account security page](https://accounts.lambdatest.com/security).
3. Copy both values.

### Step 2: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with TestMu AI.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Access Key**.
3. Click **Connect**.

You are now connected to TestMu AI.
