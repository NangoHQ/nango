## Overview

To authenticate with Datacircle, you need:

1. **API key** — the key shown on your Datacircle dashboard. One key per account.

### Prerequisites

- A Datacircle account. Sign up at [datacircle.dev/login](https://datacircle.dev/login) with a work email.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Finding your API key

1. Sign in at [datacircle.dev/login](https://datacircle.dev/login): enter your work email, then the six-digit code Datacircle emails you.
2. Your dashboard opens. The **Your API key** card shows your key, a 40-character string.
3. Click **Copy key**.

**Reset key** on the same card makes a new key; the old one stops working at once, so update the connection in Nango after a reset.

#### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the Nango Connect form for Datacircle (or **Add test connection** in the dashboard).
2. Paste your **API key**.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Datacircle.
