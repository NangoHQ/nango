## Overview

To authenticate with Doppler Marketing Automation, you need:

1. **API Key** — the API Key of your Doppler account.
2. **Account email** — the email address of your Doppler account.

This guide will show you how to get them.

### Prerequisites

- A Doppler account. [Sign up](https://app.fromdoppler.com/signup?lang=en) if you don't have one.

### Step 1: Copy your API Key

1. Log in to your [Doppler account](https://app.fromdoppler.com/).
2. Open the **Control Panel** from the account menu at the top right.
3. In the **Advanced Preferences** section, click **Doppler API**.

<img src="/api-integrations/doppler-marketing-automation/control_panel_doppler_api.png" alt="Doppler Control Panel with the Doppler API option highlighted in the Advanced Preferences section"/>

4. Copy the **API Key** shown in the orange banner at the top of the page. Doppler creates it for your account, so there is nothing to generate.

<img src="/api-integrations/doppler-marketing-automation/doppler_api_key.png" alt="Doppler API page with the API Key banner highlighted at the top"/>

### Step 2: Find your account email

Your account email is the email address you use to log in to Doppler. The Doppler API uses it to identify your account.

### Step 3: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Doppler Marketing Automation.
2. Enter your key in the **API Key** field and your login email in the **Account email** field.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/doppler-marketing-automation/form.png" alt="Doppler Marketing Automation Connect UI form with the API Key and Account email fields" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Doppler Marketing Automation.
