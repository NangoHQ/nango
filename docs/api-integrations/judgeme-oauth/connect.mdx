## Overview

To connect with Judge.me, you need:

1. **Shop domain** – The domain of the e-commerce store connected to your Judge.me account.

This guide walks you through finding this value.

### Prerequisites

- A Judge.me account with an app installed on your store.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Finding your shop domain

Your shop domain is the domain of the e-commerce store connected to Judge.me (e.g. `example.myshopify.com`). You can find it in your store admin's URL or domain settings.

#### Step 2: Authorize in Nango

1. Open the Nango Connect form.
2. Enter your **Shop domain** in the field provided.
3. Continue through the flow to log in to Judge.me and authorize access.

<img src="/api-integrations/judgeme-oauth/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>


You are now connected to Judge.me.
