## Overview

To connect with HighQ, you need:

1. **Hostname** – Your HighQ Collaborate hostname, including your instance path.

This guide walks you through finding this value.

### Prerequisites

- A HighQ Collaborate account.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Find your hostname

Your hostname is the address you use to access your Collaborate instance day to day, without the `https://` prefix. For example, if you log in at `https://acme.highq.com/main`, your hostname is `acme.highq.com/main`.

You can find it in your browser's address bar while logged in to HighQ.

#### Step 2: Authorize in Nango

1. Open the Nango Connect form.
2. Enter your **Hostname** in the field provided.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/highq/form.png" alt="Nango connection form for highq" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to HighQ.
