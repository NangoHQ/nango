title: 'Connect your Higher Logic community'
description: 'Find your community hostname and authorize access'
---

## Step 1: Find your community hostname

Use the primary hostname of your Higher Logic community, such as `yourcommunity.connectedcommunity.org`. A community may use a custom domain. Enter the hostname without `https://`, a trailing slash, or a path. Do not enter your organization's public website or a separate SSO login hostname.

The community's discovery document is available at `https://<hostname>/higherlogic/external/oauth/.well-known/openid-configuration`.

## Step 2: Authorize access

Enter the hostname in Nango's connection form and sign in through the community's login flow. The account must have permission to access the libraries and other content you want to retrieve. Complete any consent screen to return to Nango.
