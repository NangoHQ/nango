## Overview

To connect **FreshDesk (MCP)** with Nango you need:

1. **API key** — A long-lived key tied to your FreshDesk agent account.
2. **Subdomain** — The subdomain your FreshDesk account is reachable at.

This guide walks you through finding both.

### Prerequisites

- A FreshDesk account with an agent you can generate an API key for

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Finding your FreshDesk API key

1. Log in to your FreshDesk account.
2. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner and select **Profile Settings**.
3. Click **View API Key** on the right-hand side of that page to reveal your API key.

#### Step 2: Finding your FreshDesk domain

Your subdomain is the first part of the URL you use to access FreshDesk: `https://<subdomain>.freshdesk.com`.

#### Step 3: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

Once you have your **API key** and **subdomain**:

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with FreshDesk (MCP).
2. Enter both the **API key** and the **subdomain** in their respective fields.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to FreshDesk (MCP).
