## Overview

To authenticate with Databricks, you need:

1. **Cloud region** — the account console host for the cloud your Databricks account runs on.
2. **Account ID** — your Databricks account ID.

You then sign in to Databricks. This guide shows you how to find both values.

### Prerequisites

- A Databricks account. To call account-level APIs, you must sign in as an account admin.

### Step 1: Find your account console host

Pick the host of the account console you sign in to:

| Cloud | Account console host |
|-------|----------------------|
| AWS   | `accounts.cloud.databricks.com` |
| GCP   | `accounts.gcp.databricks.com` |
| Azure | `accounts.azuredatabricks.net` |

### Step 2: Find your account ID

1. Sign in to the account console for your cloud.
2. Click the down arrow next to your username in the upper right corner.
3. Copy your account ID.

### Step 3: Enter your details in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Databricks.
2. Select your **Cloud region** and enter your **Account ID**.
3. Click **Connect**. You'll be redirected to Databricks to sign in and authorize access.

<img src="/api-integrations/databricks-account-oauth/form.png" alt="Nango Connect UI form with the Cloud region and Account ID fields and the Connect button" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Databricks.
