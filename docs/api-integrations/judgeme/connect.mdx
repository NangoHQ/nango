## Overview

To authenticate with Judge.me, you will need:
1. **Shop domain** - The domain of the store your Judge.me account is connected to (e.g. a `.myshopify.com` domain).
2. **API token** - A private token that grants access to the Judge.me REST API on behalf of that store.

This guide walks you through finding both values.

### Prerequisites

- A Judge.me account with an app installed on your store.

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Finding your shop domain

Your shop domain is the domain of the e-commerce store connected to Judge.me (e.g. `example.myshopify.com`). You can find it in your store admin's URL or domain settings.

#### Step 2: Generating your API token

1. Log in to your Judge.me admin and go to **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Click **View API tokens** in the top right.

<img src="/api-integrations/judgeme/view_token.png" />

3. Copy your **Private API Token** from the credentials shown.

Contact [partnerships@judge.me](mailto:partnerships@judge.me) if you don't see the API section and need it enabled for your account.

#### Step 3: Authorize in Nango

1. Open the Nango Connect form.
2. Enter your **Shop domain** and **API token** in their respective fields.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/judgeme/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Judge.me.
