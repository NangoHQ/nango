## Overview

To authenticate with Haus, you need:

1. **Organization ID** — the ID of your Haus organization.
2. **Token ID** and **Token Secret** — the credentials of a Haus API token.

This guide will show you how to get them.

### Prerequisites

- A Haus account with access to **Token Management** in your organization settings.

### Step 1: Find your organization ID

Your Organization ID is shown in Haus when you connect your store to Attribution with the Haus Pixel. Copy it from there.

<Tip>
If you can't find your Organization ID, contact your Haus account team.
</Tip>

### Step 2: Create an API token

1. Log in to [Haus](https://app.haus.io).
2. Open the account menu and select **Organization settings**.
3. Select the **Token Management** tab.
4. In the **Haus Public API** section, click **Add Token**.
5. Enter a **Token Name**, using only lowercase letters, numbers, underscores, and dashes, then click **Create Token**.
6. Copy the **token_id** and **token_secret**.

<Note>
Haus only shows the token_id and token_secret once. Save them somewhere secure before closing the dialog.
</Note>

### Step 3: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Haus.
2. Enter your **Organization ID**, your token_id as the **Token ID**, and your token_secret as the **Token Secret**.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/haus/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Haus.
