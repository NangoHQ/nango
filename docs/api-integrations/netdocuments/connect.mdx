## Overview

To connect with NetDocuments, you need:

1. **Data center hostname** — the NetDocuments data center your repository lives in.

This guide will show you how to find it.

### Prerequisites

- A NetDocuments account.
- Your repository administrator has authorized the app for your repository.

### Step 1: Find your data center

Pick the data center that matches the address you use to sign in to NetDocuments:

| Region | Data center hostname |
| - | - |
| United States | `vault.netvoyage.com` |
| Europe | `eu.netdocuments.com` |
| Australia | `au.netdocuments.com` |
| Germany | `de.netdocuments.com` |
| Canada | `can.netdocuments.com` |

If you're not sure, ask your NetDocuments administrator.

### Step 2: Enter your data center in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with NetDocuments.
2. Select your **Data center hostname**.
3. Submit the form, then sign in to NetDocuments and approve access.

<img src="/api-integrations/netdocuments/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to NetDocuments.
