## Overview

To authenticate with Microsoft (Admin - Certificate), you need:

1. **Tenant ID** - The unique identifier for your organization that uses Microsoft services.

You also need to sign in as an administrator of that tenant to grant the app's permissions for your organization.

### Prerequisites

- A Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) tenant, and an account that can grant admin consent in it (for example a Global Administrator, Cloud Application Administrator or Application Administrator).

### Step 1: Finding your Tenant ID

1. Sign in to the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com).
2. Open **Identity** > **Overview**. Your **Tenant ID** is shown on the **Overview** tab.

<img src="/integrations/all/microsoft-business-central/tenant_id.png" />

### Step 2: Enter your Tenant ID in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Microsoft (Admin - Certificate).
2. Enter your **Tenant ID**.
3. Submit the form. Microsoft opens a sign-in page: sign in as an administrator and click **Accept** to grant the permissions for your organization.

<img src="/api-integrations/microsoft-admin-cert/form.png" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Microsoft (Admin - Certificate).
