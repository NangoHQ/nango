## Overview

To authenticate with Rev AI, you need:

1. **Region** - the Rev AI deployment your account is on.
2. **Access Token** - a Rev AI access token.

This guide will show you how to find both.

### Prerequisites

- A Rev AI account. If you don't have one, [sign up for a free account](https://www.rev.ai/auth/signup).

### Step 1: Find your region

Rev AI runs two deployments, each with its own API host:

| Region | Host |
| --- | --- |
| US | `api.rev.ai` |
| EU (Frankfurt) | `ec1.api.rev.ai` |

The flag under the Rev AI logo in the dashboard sidebar shows your deployment. Pick `api.rev.ai` unless Rev AI support or your Customer Success Manager set up EU access for your account. A token used against the wrong deployment is denied access.

### Step 2: Generate an access token

1. Log in to [Rev AI](https://www.rev.ai/auth/login).
2. In the left sidebar, click **Access Token**.

<img src="/api-integrations/rev-ai/access_token_nav.png" alt="Rev AI dashboard sidebar with the Access Token link highlighted"/>

3. Click **Generate** (or **Generate New Access Token** if you already have a token).

<img src="/api-integrations/rev-ai/generate_access_token.png" alt="Rev AI Access Token page with the Generate button highlighted"/>

4. In the **Generate new access token** dialog, click **Generate New Token**.

<img src="/api-integrations/rev-ai/confirm_generate_token.png" alt="Rev AI dialog to confirm generating a new access token"/>

5. Copy the **New Access Token** right away and store it somewhere safe.

<img src="/api-integrations/rev-ai/access_token_created.png" alt="Rev AI Access Token page showing the new access token field"/>

<Note>
Rev AI shows each access token only once, and an account can have at most 2 access tokens at a time. If you lose a token, generate a new one and delete the old one.
</Note>

### Step 3: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Rev AI.
2. Select your **Region** and enter your **Access Token**.
3. Click **Connect**. Nango checks the token with Rev AI before saving the connection.

<img src="/api-integrations/rev-ai/form.png" alt="Nango Connect UI form for choosing the Rev AI region and entering the access token" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Rev AI.
