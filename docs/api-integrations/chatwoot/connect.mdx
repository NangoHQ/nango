## Overview

To authenticate with Chatwoot, you need the following:
1. **Hostname** - The hostname of your Chatwoot instance (`app.chatwoot.com` for Chatwoot Cloud, or your own domain if self-hosted).
2. **Account ID** - The numeric ID of the Chatwoot account you want to connect.
3. **Access Token** - The personal access token of a Chatwoot user.

This guide walks you through finding these values in Chatwoot.

### Prerequisites

- Access to a Chatwoot account, either on Chatwoot Cloud or on a self-hosted instance.

<Note>
API calls run with the permissions of the user whose access token you use. An administrator token gives access to the whole account; an agent token is limited to the inboxes that agent can see.
</Note>

### Instructions

#### Step 1: Finding your hostname and account ID

Log in to Chatwoot and look at your browser's address bar. It looks like this:

https://app.chatwoot.com/app/accounts/12345/dashboard

- Your hostname is the domain: **`app.chatwoot.com`**.
- Your account ID is the number after `/accounts/`: **`12345`**.

#### Step 2: Generating an access token

1. Log in to your **Chatwoot** account.
2. Click on your **avatar** in the bottom-left corner and select **Profile settings**.
3. Scroll down to the **Access Token** section.
4. **Copy the access token** and store it securely.

#### Step 3: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

Once you have your credentials:

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Chatwoot.
2. Enter the **Hostname**, **Account ID** and **Access Token** in their designated fields.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Chatwoot.
