## Overview

To authenticate with DaData.ru, you need:

1. **API key** — the API key from your DaData profile.
2. **Secret key** — the secret key from your DaData profile.

This guide will show you where to find both.

### Prerequisites

- A DaData.ru account. If you don't have one, register at [dadata.ru](https://dadata.ru/).

### Step 1: Confirm your email

1. Go to [dadata.ru](https://dadata.ru/) and click **Подключиться** to register with your name, email and password.
2. Open the **Регистрация на DaData.ru** email and click the confirmation link.

DaData generates your API key and secret key automatically once your email is confirmed. The keys don't work until you confirm it.

### Step 2: Copy your API key and secret key

1. Open your profile at [dadata.ru/profile](https://dadata.ru/profile/#info) and select the **Почта, ключи, деньги** tab.
2. In the **Ключи** section, copy the **API-ключ** (API key) and the **Секретный ключ** (secret key) with their copy buttons.

<img src="/api-integrations/dadata-ru/profile_api_keys.png" alt="DaData profile page, Почта, ключи, деньги tab, with the Ключи section holding the API-ключ and Секретный ключ fields and their copy buttons highlighted"/>

### Step 3: Enter your keys in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with DaData.ru.
2. Enter the **API-ключ** value in the **API key** field.
3. Enter the **Секретный ключ** value in the **Secret key** field.
4. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/dadata-ru/form.png" alt="DaData.ru Connect UI form with the API key and Secret key fields" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to DaData.ru.
