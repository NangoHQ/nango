## Overview

Feedbin's API uses your account login, so there is no API key to create. To authenticate with Feedbin, you need:

1. **Email** — the email address of your Feedbin account.
2. **Password** — the password of your Feedbin account.

This guide will show you where to find them.

### Prerequisites

- A Feedbin account. You can [sign up](https://feedbin.com/signup) for a 30-day free trial.

### Step 1: Find your account email

1. Log in to [Feedbin](https://feedbin.com/login).
2. Go to **Settings** > [Account](https://feedbin.com/settings/account). The **Email** field shows your account email. You'll enter it in the **Email** field of the Connect UI.

<img src="/api-integrations/feedbin/account_email.png" alt="Feedbin Settings Account page with the Email field highlighted" />

### Step 2: Enter your credentials in the Connect UI

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Feedbin.
2. Enter your Feedbin account email in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your Feedbin account password in the **Password** field.
4. Submit the form. Nango checks your credentials with Feedbin, and you should be successfully authenticated.

<img src="/api-integrations/feedbin/form.png" alt="Feedbin Connect UI form with the Email and Password fields" style={{maxWidth: "450px" }}/>

You are now connected to Feedbin.
