This guide shows you how to create an OAuth client for Oracle Aconex to obtain your OAuth credentials (client ID & secret). These are required to let your users grant your app access to their Aconex account.

Aconex uses the Oracle Construction and Engineering Lobby as its OAuth server.

<Steps>
  <Step id="open-oauth-clients" title="Open OAuth Clients in the Lobby">
    Log in to the [Oracle Construction and Engineering Lobby](https://constructionandengineering.oraclecloud.com) and select **OAuth Clients** from the main menu.

    <Note>
    Only Lobby Admins can create OAuth clients.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step id="create-oauth-client" title="Create an OAuth client">
    Click **Add** and fill in:
    - **Name** — a unique, meaningful name, such as your organization or integration name.
    - **Description** — what the integration does.
    - **Resource Application** — **Aconex** (the default).
    - **Type** — **Web Server Application**.
    - **Redirect URL** — `https://api.nango.dev/oauth/callback`
    - **Organizations** — the organizations to provision the client to.

    Check the box to accept the terms of use, then click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step id="copy-credentials" title="Copy your credentials">
    Find your new OAuth client in the list and click the eye icon to reveal the **Client Secret**. Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**, then enter them in your Oracle Aconex integration in Nango.
  </Step>
  <Step id="configure-sso-policies" title="Allow the integration in your identity domain (SSO or 2SV only)">
    Skip this step unless you've configured single sign-on (SSO) or two-step verification (2SV) in your own Identity Domain.

    - **SSO** — go to **Security** > **IdP Policies**, select your policy, click **Apps** > **Add app**, then search for and select your OAuth client.
    - **2SV** — go to **Security** > **MFA** > **Sign-on policies**, select your organization's policy, click **Add app**, then search for and select your OAuth client.
  </Step>
  <Step id="next" title="Next">
    Follow the [_Quickstart_](/getting-started/quickstart) to connect your first account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details, see [Create an OAuth Client](https://help.aconex.com/apis/create-an-oauth-client/) and [Implement OAuth](https://help.aconex.com/apis/implement-smart-construction-platform-oauth/) in Aconex Support Central.
