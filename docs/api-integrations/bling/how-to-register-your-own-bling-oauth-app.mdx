This guide shows you how to create a Bling app to get your OAuth credentials (client ID & secret). Your users then authorize your app to access their Bling account through the API v3.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Bling account">
    If you don't have a Bling account, [sign up](https://www.bling.com.br) for one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an app">
    1. In Bling, open the **Central de Extensões** and click **Área do integrador**.
    2. Click **Criar aplicativo** and fill in the app's basic data.
    3. Set the **Link de redirecionamento** to `https://api.nango.dev/oauth/callback` (or your own [custom callback URL](/guides/auth/auth-guide#custom-oauth-callback-url-optional)). A Bling app has a single redirect URL.
    4. Choose the scopes your integration needs, for example products to list products.
    5. Save, then copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** from the **Informações do app** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the credentials in Nango">
    In your Bling integration in Nango, paste the client ID and secret. Leave scopes empty: Bling applies the scopes chosen on the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next">
    Follow the [_Quickstart_](/getting-started/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

For more details, see [Bling's API v3 documentation](https://developer.bling.com.br/aplicativos).
