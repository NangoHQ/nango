## Overview

To authenticate with Heabsy, you will need:
1. **API Key** - A key that authenticates your requests to the Heabsy API.

This guide will walk you through creating your **API Key** in the Heabsy console.

### Prerequisites:

- A Heabsy account. You can sign in or create one at [platform.heabsy.com](https://platform.heabsy.com).

### Instructions:

#### Step 1: Create your API key

1. Sign in at [platform.heabsy.com](https://platform.heabsy.com).
2. Create a new API key in the console and give it a name.
3. Copy the key and store it securely.

<Warning>Anyone holding this key can make requests on your Heabsy account.</Warning>

#### Step 2: Enter credentials in the Connect UI

Once you have your **API Key**:

1. Open the form where you need to authenticate with Heabsy.
2. Enter your **API Key** in its respective field.
3. Submit the form, and you should be successfully authenticated.

You are now connected to Heabsy.
